About FreeCVResume.co.in

FreeCVResume.co.in is a web platform bringing together practical career tools and educational resources. The site includes a resume builder, photo utility, typing practice and a study-notes area where creators can submit material they have the rights to share.

Our goal

We aim to make useful learning and career tools easier to access on mobile and desktop. Marketplace features are being developed in stages; availability of accounts, purchases, creator earnings and other services may vary until their secure backend integrations are enabled.

Content responsibility

Creators are responsible for the rights and accuracy of their uploads. Readers should review educational material critically and respect the creator’s license and copyright notices.
