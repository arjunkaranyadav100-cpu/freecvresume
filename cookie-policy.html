Cookie Policy

This page explains cookies and similar technologies on FreeCVResume.co.in.

What technologies may be used?

Our tools may use local storage, session storage and IndexedDB for saved notes, preferences, typing history, reading progress and temporary PDF caching. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and personalization.

Managing choices

When you first visit, the website displays a choice panel for optional advertising cookies. You can accept or reject these cookies, and later reopen the choice panel using “Cookie Settings” in the footer. Your selection is stored in this browser. If you accept, the website loads its Google AdSense script; if you reject, the website does not intentionally load that script through the site preference control. You can also manage or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking storage may affect saved notes, login state, reading progress and other features. Where consent is legally required, applicable consent choices must be presented and respected before the relevant non-essential technologies are used.

Advertising privacy

For details about Google advertising technologies, visit Google’s advertising policies and Google’s partner-site data disclosure. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may require a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF when personalized ads are served.

The website’s own preference panel is not a Google-certified CMP and does not by itself satisfy the IAB TCF requirement for personalized ads in the EEA, UK or Switzerland. Before serving personalized ads in those regions, the operator must configure Google Privacy & Messaging or another Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF. A written Cookie Policy alone is not a CMP.
