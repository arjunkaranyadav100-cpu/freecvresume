Privacy Policy

FreeCVResume.co.in (“we”, “our”, or “the website”) provides resume tools, typing practice and a study-notes marketplace. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the website.

Information you provide

Depending on the feature, you may enter resume details, contact details, account credentials, creator profile information, UPI ID, PDF files, note descriptions, search terms, bookmarks and reading progress. Resume content you enter in the resume builder is processed in your browser for the feature to work. Do not enter sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

Browser storage and cloud services

The website may use browser local storage, session storage, IndexedDB and similar technologies to remember preferences, typing history, saved notes, reading progress and cached PDF files. Creator uploads and marketplace records may be sent to our configured cloud/database and file-storage providers when those services are enabled. Information stored only on your device may be lost if you clear browser data or change devices.

Advertising and third parties

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements after approval and configuration. Google and other advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers to serve, measure and personalize ads, subject to their policies and your choices. Third-party providers may collect or receive information through their technologies. Learn how Google uses data when you use partner sites and review Google’s Privacy Policy.

Why information is used

Information may be used to operate tools, store and display notes, maintain accounts, protect the service, prevent fraud and abuse, respond to support requests, improve performance, and support advertising where enabled. We do not promise that every feature is currently connected to a production cloud service.

Sharing and retention

Information may be processed by hosting, database, file-storage, security, analytics, payment or advertising providers when those services are enabled. We do not sell personal information as a standalone data-broker product. Data is retained only as needed for the relevant feature, legal obligations, security and dispute handling; actual retention can vary by provider and browser storage.

Your choices and requests

You can avoid submitting optional information, clear browser storage, and contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we control, subject to legal and operational limits. Deleting browser data does not necessarily delete cloud records, and deleting cloud records does not clear your device cache.

Children

The website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features and providers change. For privacy questions or requests, use our contact page.

This policy is a general website disclosure, not legal advice. Before enabling advertising, accounts, uploads or payments for the public, the operator should verify that every statement matches the actual deployed data flows and applicable law.
