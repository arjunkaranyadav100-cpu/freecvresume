Copyright & Content Policy

FreeCVResume.co.in respects intellectual-property rights and expects creators to do the same. Upload only content you own or are expressly authorized to distribute.

Prohibited uploads

Do not upload pirated books, paid coaching notes, scans of copyrighted textbooks, leaked examination material, copied course packs or content that misuses another person’s name, logo, watermark or work.

Submit a copyright complaint

A complaint should identify the copyrighted work, the exact URL or title of the material on our website, your name and contact details, a statement explaining your ownership or authority to act, and a good-faith statement that the use is not authorized. Send it using the contact details on our Contact page.

Review and action

We may temporarily restrict access while reviewing a credible complaint, ask the uploader for proof of rights, remove or disable the reported material when appropriate, and take action against repeat infringers. False or abusive complaints may be rejected.

Counter-notice

If your material was removed in error, contact us with the URL, an explanation and evidence of your rights or authorization. We may restore access where appropriate and legally permitted.

This page describes an intended reporting process. A staffed inbox, response timelines, evidence review and admin takedown workflow must be operational before the marketplace is publicly opened at scale.
